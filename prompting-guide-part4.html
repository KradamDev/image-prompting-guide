Section 01 — Photography-Style Prompts
01 / PHOTOGRAPHY-STYLE PROMPTS

Portraits, Food & Animals

Photography-style prompts represent one of the most practically useful categories in AI image generation, and also one of the most technically specific. The goal is to produce an image that does not merely depict a subject but convincingly appears to have been captured through a real camera lens. Achieving this requires a vocabulary drawn from professional photography: camera models, aperture and shutter speed values, ISO ratings, lens focal lengths, and lighting setups. These parameters do not instruct the AI on optics — the AI has no camera — but they communicate the aesthetic signature of the settings they describe, drawing on patterns the model has learned from millions of tagged photographs.

The fundamental formula for any photography-style prompt is: subject + camera model + technical settings + lighting descriptor + quality tags. Each genre within photography then applies its own characteristic variants to this formula.

Portrait Photography
Photorealistic
well-lit, sharp-focus, high-quality, artistic, unique, award-winning photograph, Canon EOS 5D Mark IV DSLR, f/8, ISO 100, 1/250 second, close-up, natural light, professional, flattering, headshot, glibatree style
Negative: grainy, deformed
Camera and technical settings — specifying a recognisable camera body and realistic exposure values (f/8, ISO 100, 1/250s) communicates the sharp, controlled aesthetic of professional studio portraiture.
Lighting and framing — natural light combined with close-up and headshot directs both the illumination quality and the compositional distance from the subject simultaneously.
Style attribution — phrases like award-winning photograph and named style references signal the calibre and aesthetic lineage the AI should emulate.
Food Photography
Commercial
5 Delicious Food Photography:4 well-lit, sharp-focus, high-quality, artistic, unique, award-winning photograph, Canon EOS 5D Mark IV DSLR, f/8 aperture, 1/125 second shutter speed, ISO 100, professional lighting setup, Adobe Photoshop, appetizing composition, attention to detail
Negative: blurry, boring, generic, unappetizing, simple:-2, grainy, smokey
Batch specification — the 5 ... :4 syntax requests five variations at quality level 4, producing a set of images suitable for selecting the strongest composition rather than committing to a single generation.
Domain-specific descriptors — appetizing composition and professional lighting setup activate the model's understanding of food styling conventions: hero angles, controlled highlights, and colour saturation that makes food look edible and appealing.
Negative prompt weighting — simple:-2 applies a weighted negative, pushing especially hard against flat or under-composed results. This is the same emphasis mechanism from Part I, applied within the undesired content field.
Animal Photography
Pet & Wildlife
adorable pet photography, cute, well-lit, sharp-focus, high-quality, artistic, unique, award-winning photograph, Canon EOS 5D Mark IV DSLR, f/8, ISO 100, 1/250 second, close-up, natural light, domestic animals, playful, fluffy, glibatree style
Subject framing — opening with adorable pet photography rather than simply "a dog" immediately positions the image within a specific commercial genre, activating the model's understanding of the shallow depth-of-field, warm colour grading, and intimate framing that characterise professional pet portraiture.
Personality tags — playful and fluffy contribute to the emotional register of the image rather than its technical properties, guiding the model toward a warmer, more expressive compositional choice.
💡
When prompting for photography-style images, the camera model tag does not need to be technically accurate to your desired output. Its primary function is to signal a quality tier and aesthetic convention to the model. Canon EOS 5D Mark IV and Sony Alpha a7R IV consistently produce high-fidelity results because they appear frequently in high-quality training data — not because the AI understands their sensor specifications.
Section 02 — Architecture & Environments
02 / ARCHITECTURE & ENVIRONMENTS

Spaces, Structures & Scenes

Architectural and environmental prompts operate on a different axis from character or photography prompts. Rather than describing a subject within a scene, they are the scene — the built or natural environment is the entire image. The challenge here is conveying not just what the space looks like, but its physical properties: material behaviour under light, structural logic, spatial scale, and the relationship between constructed and natural elements.

Professional architectural rendering has a well-established visual vocabulary — one the model has absorbed from years of exposure to architectural photography, 3D studio renders, and design publications. Drawing on that vocabulary deliberately produces far more convincing results than describing a building in lay terms.

Professional Architecture Render
3D Render
5 Professional Architecture Render:4 photorealistic, 3D rendering, high-quality, detailed, accurate representation, 3D Studio Max, V-Ray, professional, corporate, award-winning, glibatree style, realistic materials, accurate dimensions, multiple angles, perspective views:3
Negative: deformed, abstract, unrealistic materials, incorrect dimensions, flat views:-2
Rendering software references — naming 3D Studio Max and V-Ray signals the precise quality tier of professional architectural visualisation: correct material response to light, physically accurate shadows, and the clean, polished finish associated with these industry-standard tools.
Material and dimension accuracy — these tags specifically counter the model's tendency to produce aesthetically plausible but structurally incoherent architecture, where windows float, proportions distort, or materials behave incorrectly under the depicted lighting.
Multi-view request — multiple angles and perspective views:3 instructs the model to present the structure from several vantage points within a single image — a standard deliverable in professional architectural presentations.
Surreal / Imaginative Environment
Conceptual
big whale playing in the middle of the desert, high quality, high resolution, high detail, realistic, epic cinematic, --q 2, --v 5
Conceptual clarity through contradiction — the power of this prompt lies in the precise collision of two incongruous realities: the desert and the whale. The model understands both environments and both subjects with high fidelity; placing them in contact produces a striking surrealist image precisely because each half is rendered realistically.
Platform parameters — --q 2 and --v 5 are Midjourney-specific flags controlling quality level and model version respectively. On platforms using this syntax, these parameters are appended at the end of the prompt and significantly shape output fidelity.
Environment Prompt Variations // Futuristic performance centre — parametric architecture square roof, parametric design, architecture, futuristic art performance center, high quality, 8k, --q 2, --v 5 // Flooded ancient city — epic scale Egyptian old city under the water, high quality, high resolution, high detail, realistic, epic cinematic, 8k ultra details, --q 2, --v 5 // Desert luxury dining — landscape photography style 5 stars restaurant in the desert, rocky and sandy terrain, beautiful landscape, landscape photography, depth of view, Fujifilm GFX 100, --uplight 8k
"The most memorable environmental prompts share a structural characteristic: they are internally coherent even when conceptually radical. A flooded Egyptian city is surreal, but the individual elements — water, ancient stone, atmospheric haze — are each rendered with documentary precision. Surrealism built on realistic foundations consistently outperforms vague fantasy."
Section 03 — Sci-Fi & Fantasy
03 / SCI-FI & FANTASY

Speculative & Fantastical Imagery

Science fiction and fantasy are among the categories where AI image generation excels most dramatically — and where prompting discipline matters most. Both genres are broad enough to encompass radically different visual traditions, from the clean minimalism of hard science fiction to the maximalist ornamentation of high fantasy. Without specific directional tags, the model defaults to the most statistically common representations of each genre, which tend to be generic. The key to distinctive results is the same specificity that separates strong prompts in any genre, applied now to the particular conventions of speculative visual storytelling.

Sci-Fi — Humanoid Android
Fantasy / Sci-Fi
humanoid android, covered in white porcelain skin, blue eyes, white wispy ghost, wearing ornate armour, 4k realistic fantasy, insane details, ghost phantom effect, hyper detailed, photoreal, photography, cinematic lighting, hdr, hd, cinematography, realism, fine art digital, HD, Mark Molnar mystical, redshift rendering, 8k, --q 2, --v 5
Material contradiction as design principle — pairing white porcelain skin with ghost phantom effect defines the character's visual identity through a deliberate material paradox: solid yet translucent, artificial yet ethereal. This specificity prevents the model from falling back on generic android conventions.
Artist and renderer references — Mark Molnar mystical and redshift rendering invoke a specific illustrator's stylistic approach and a production-quality rendering engine, respectively. Both function as strong aesthetic anchors that orient the model toward a precise visual territory.
Quality stacking — layering hyper detailed, photoreal, cinematography, and 8k in close succession signals unambiguously that this image should be rendered at the highest possible level of fidelity — each tag reinforcing the same quality requirement from a different directional angle.
Sci-Fi — Interior Environment
3D Render
5 Futuristic Sci-Fi 3D Render::4 high-tech, science fiction, neon lighting, intriguing, imaginitive futuristic, detailed, realistic, 3D rendering, metallic, digital art, Unreal Engine 6, 3D Studio Max, V-Ray, award-winning, glibatree style:3 Negative: deformed, abstract:-2
Game engine as quality signal — Unreal Engine 6 functions similarly to a rendering software reference in the architecture prompt: it communicates real-time global illumination, physically-based material rendering, and the high-fidelity visual standard associated with AAA game production environments.
Neon lighting as genre anchor — neon lighting is one of the most efficient single tags for steering the model toward the atmospheric interior aesthetic that defines science fiction visual culture, evoking the genre's characteristic blue-purple-pink colour palette without requiring explicit colour specification.
Fantasy Robot — Field Environment // Destroyed ancient Egypt setting — sci-fi meets archaeology a robot standing straight in front of the ruins of a destroyed ancient Egypt. No other character on the image. Warm colors. The robot is photographed from behind with an a6400 24mm 8k
🔬
For sci-fi and fantasy prompts, contrast between the familiar and the impossible is the most reliable path to a compelling image. A robot photographed from behind with a specific real-world camera and lens specification, standing in front of a recognisable historical ruin, produces a far more arresting result than a prompt that is uniformly fantastical throughout. The real-world anchor makes the impossible element feel genuinely strange rather than merely invented.
Section 04 — Character Art & Illustration
04 / CHARACTER ART & ILLUSTRATION

3D Characters, Cartoons & Concept Art

Character art and illustration prompts differ from the photorealistic genres in one crucial respect: the image is expected to look created rather than captured. Rather than invoking cameras and exposure settings, these prompts invoke the software, stylistic traditions, and production pipelines of the digital art industry. The model has been trained on enormous quantities of digital illustration, concept art, 3D character renders, and stylised animation — and it responds with high precision to the vocabulary of those disciplines.

3D Character Concept Art
Digital Art
5 Character Concept Art:4 creative, expressive, detailed, colorful, stylized anatomy, digital art, 3D rendering, unique, award-winning, Adobe Photoshop, 3D Studio Max, V-Ray, professional, glibatree style, well-developed concept, distinct personality, consistent style:3
Negative: deformed, simple, undeveloped concept, generic personality, inconsistent styles:-2
Stylized anatomy as an intentional directive — explicitly requesting stylized anatomy signals that anatomical accuracy is secondary to aesthetic expressiveness. This prevents the model from defaulting to photorealistic proportions when producing characters that belong more to the animation or graphic novel tradition.
Personality as a visual property — distinct personality and well-developed concept are abstract instructions that nonetheless produce measurable visual differences. The model interprets these as signals to introduce more individualised detail in facial expression, posture, costume design, and colour palette rather than generating a generically appealing character.
Negative weighting for concept integrity — generic personality:-2 and inconsistent styles:-2 in the negative prompt push hard against the model's statistical tendency toward aesthetically average, stylistically muddled character designs.
Cartoon / Comic-Style
Illustration
creative, expressive, detailed, colorful, stylized anatomy, high-quality, digital art, 3D rendering, stylized, unique, award-winning, Adobe Photoshop, 3D Studio Max, V-Ray, playful, fantastical:2
Negative: plain background, simple, deformed
Weighted style emphasis — fantastical:2 applies an inline weight multiplier, instructing the model to lean strongly into imaginative, dreamlike visual properties. This is equivalent to wrapping the tag in double emphasis brackets {"{{}}"} in tag-based syntax.
Targeted negative prompt — plain background specifically prevents the model's common shortcut of rendering cartoon-style figures against a simple white or gradient fill, which tends to look unfinished in this genre.
Vintage Album Art — Editorial
Graphic Design
A vintage-inspired album cover for a rock musician, incorporating moody lighting and classic design elements, influenced by the works of Reid Miles --ar 2:3 --s 400 8k --q 2 --v 5
Named artistic influence — referencing Reid Miles — the celebrated Blue Note Records designer — provides the model with an extremely precise aesthetic anchor: high-contrast black and white photography, bold typographic layouts, modernist geometry, and deliberate negative space. A single artist name can be more directive than a dozen descriptive adjectives.
Aspect ratio as format specification — --ar 2:3 produces a portrait-oriented canvas appropriate for album cover artwork, reinforcing the editorial intent of the prompt at the compositional level. Matching the aspect ratio to the intended end use is a habit that consistently improves results.
⚠️
When referencing named artists or specific stylistic traditions, always verify whether the model is likely to have strong training data for that reference. A deeply specialised or historically obscure artist reference may produce unpredictable results if that artist's work is underrepresented in the model's training. In such cases, describe the stylistic properties in explicit visual terms rather than relying on the name alone.
Section 05 — Building Your Master Formula
05 / YOUR MASTER FORMULA

A Universal Prompt Architecture

Having examined how prompts function across six distinct genres — portraiture, food, animals, architecture, sci-fi, and character illustration — a set of universal structural patterns becomes apparent. Every strong prompt, regardless of genre, is built from the same underlying components arranged in the same logical order. The genre determines which specific tags populate each component; the architecture itself remains constant. Understanding this universal architecture is the final conceptual step in moving from responsive to deliberate image generation.

Each component of the formula can be as minimal or as elaborate as the genre demands. A portrait prompt requires significant investment in the technical reference component (camera body, exposure settings, lighting). An architectural render requires detail in both the style component (rendering software, material properties) and the technical component (angle, scale). A surreal environment prompt may compress subject and setting into a single evocative phrase. The formula is not a rigid template — it is a checklist that ensures no dimension of the image is left undefined.

From Formula to Masterwork — A Final Checklist

Before submitting any prompt for final generation, running through the following checklist takes under thirty seconds and consistently improves output quality. Each question targets a different layer of the formula, ensuring that no component has been left under-specified or accidentally omitted.

  • Is the subject stated explicitly and early? Confirm that the most important element of the image — the character, object, or scene — appears in the first few tags, not buried mid-prompt where its influence is diminished.

  • Is the genre communicated through specific vocabulary? Replace generic descriptors ("beautiful," "nice") with genre-native terms ("appetizing composition" for food, "cinematic lighting" for portraiture, "parametric design" for architecture).

  • Is there a technical reference? Photography genres need a camera model; 3D render genres need a software reference; illustration genres need a style or tool attribution. Without this anchor, the model selects its own technical frame — which may not align with your intent.

  • Are emphasis brackets applied where needed? Review the prompt for any element whose absence or incorrectness would constitute a failure of the image. Apply {{}} to those elements to ensure they receive priority during generation.

  • Is the Undesired Content field populated? Add at minimum blurry, bad anatomy, and genre-specific failure modes (such as unappetizing for food or flat views for architecture) before generating.

  • Does the canvas aspect ratio match the compositional intent? A mismatched canvas will force the model into awkward cropping decisions that undermine even a well-structured prompt.

One More Prompt — Putting It All Together
A Fully Annotated Final Example
Complete Prompt // Industrial living room — editorial photography / interior design photo of an industrial and gothic style living room, with a high ceiling and a large bay window, armchairs and black chesterfield sofa, with a glamorous 60s style decoration with posters of old movies from the 30s on the wall --s 1000 --s 30000 --q 2 --s 750 --q 2 --v 5
Period contradiction as atmospheric depth — the prompt layers three distinct historical references (industrial/gothic architecture, 1960s glamour, 1930s cinema) into a single space. This deliberate anachronism is not a mistake — it is the visual concept of the image, and the model interprets the collision of specific eras as a rich, textured interior rather than a confused one.
Specific furnishing details — naming black chesterfield sofa rather than simply "sofa" and large bay window rather than "window" provides the model with enough specificity to construct a coherent, recognisable interior rather than a generic room.
